# My Hope Story

My Hope Story is a digital storytelling platform for entrepreneurs who experienced startup failure. It transforms failed ventures into learning assets, community-backed support, and potential revival opportunities.

## Vision

Every failed startup contains knowledge worth sharing and ideas worth saving.

## Mission

Enable founders to document their complete journey, preserve lessons, and connect with mentors, investors, and the community.

## What the platform offers

- Structured failure storytelling
- Community learning and mentorship
- Donation, crowdfunding, and angel investment pathways
- Moderation and verification for credible founder stories
- Analytics for investors, mentors, and educators
