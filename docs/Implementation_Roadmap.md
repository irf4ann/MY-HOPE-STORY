# Implementation Roadmap

## 1. Project Phases

### Phase 1: Discovery and Planning

- Validate concept and user personas
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Build wireframes and initial UX flows
- Establish technology architecture and stack

### Phase 2: MVP Build

- User registration and authentication
- Entrepreneur profile and story submission flow
- Story moderation and publish workflow
- Basic search and story browsing
- Community interaction: comments and likes
- Donation module with payment integration

### Phase 3: Grow and Validate

- Add crowdfunding campaign support
- Investor and mentor connection flows
- Recommendation engine for stories and experts
- Expanded analytics dashboards
- Mobile-ready responsive design
- Moderation tools and fraud detection

### Phase 4: Scale and Expand

- Mobile app launch
- Grant and incubator recommendation engine
- AI-powered story summarization and insights
- Multi-language support
- Strategic partnerships with incubators and accelerators

## 2. Timeline (12 months)

- Months 1–2: research, requirements, wireframes
- Months 3–5: MVP development and internal testing
- Month 6: soft launch with pilot users
- Months 7–8: feedback iteration and community growth
- Months 9–10: launch crowdfunding and investor features
- Months 11–12: scale operations, partnerships, and mobile app

## 3. Key Milestones

- M1: Completed BRD, FRD, and UX prototypes
- M2: MVP launch with live story publishing
- M3: First 100 stories published and first donor transaction
- M4: Mentor and investor onboarding launch
- M5: First revived business supported through platform

## 4. Risk Mitigation

- Moderate content before publication to prevent fake stories
- Use secure payments and fraud prevention for funding flows
- Start with a focused initial market vertical to drive quality
- Maintain founder privacy options to encourage participation

## 5. Go-to-Market Strategy

- Partner with startup incubators and entrepreneurship communities
- Launch campaigns targeted at failed founders and student programs
- Publish success case studies and community testimonials
- Use content marketing around founder failure lessons

## 6. KPI Roadmap

- Launch: 500 registered users, 100 stories, 20 mentors
- Growth: 1,000 monthly active users, 300 stories, ₹10 lakh funding interest
- Scale: 10,000 users, 1,000 stories, 50 revived businesses

## 7. Governance and Operations

- Establish moderation team and policy framework
- Create advisory board with investors and mentors
- Define periodic review cadence for product metrics
- Implement customer support and dispute resolution processes
