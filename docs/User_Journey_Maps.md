# User Journey Maps

## 1. Entrepreneur Journey

### Steps

1. Register or sign in.
2. Create founder profile.
3. Write and structure a failed startup story.
4. Upload media, documents, and evidence.
5. Submit story for review.
6. Track review status and address feedback.
7. Published story receives comments and likes.
8. Receive mentorship requests or funding interest.
9. Apply for revival support or crowdfunding.
10. Restart or iterate the business with community support.

### Key Touchpoints

- Onboarding flow
- Story creation wizard
- Review feedback interface
- Notifications for engagement and funding
- Mentor/investor match dashboard

## 2. Investor Journey

### Steps

1. Sign up and verify investor profile.
2. Browse failure stories by category and filter.
3. Save promising stories and founders.
4. Review verified funding requests and lessons learned.
5. Express interest or request a direct connection.
6. Receive founder response and due diligence data.
7. Support a founder with funding or introduction.

### Key Touchpoints

- Search and recommendation engine
- Story credibility indicators
- Investor interest forms
- Secure messaging gateway

## 3. Mentor Journey

### Steps

1. Register as mentor and define expertise.
2. Discover founders needing guidance.
3. Review story details and failure context.
4. Offer mentorship or advice publicly.
5. Schedule sessions with founders.
6. Track impact and follow up on progress.

### Key Touchpoints

- Mentor dashboard
- Suggested founder matches
- Session scheduling and notes
- Outcome tracking

## 4. Student/Researcher Journey

### Steps

1. Browse curated stories and case studies.
2. Filter by industry, failure reason, or geography.
3. Read structured lessons and timeline summaries.
4. Bookmark stories for later study.
5. Share insights with peers or in coursework.

### Key Touchpoints

- Story categories and filters
- Lesson summary cards
- Bookmark and reading list
- Export or share insights
