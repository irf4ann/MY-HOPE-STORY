# Moderation Policy and Framework

## 1. Moderation Principles

- Encourage authentic founder storytelling.
- Protect users from fraud, hate, and misinformation.
- Preserve community trust with transparent review criteria.
- Balance open sharing with content quality and safety.

## 2. Content Categories

### Allowed

- Real entrepreneurial failure experiences
- Evidence-based claims and timelines
- Constructive lessons and reflections
- Respectful feedback and mentorship

### Disallowed

- Fake or fabricated startup stories
- Plagiarism and copied content
- Hate speech, discrimination, and personal attacks
- False or misleading fundraising requests
- Fraud, scams, and illegal activity

## 3. Review Workflow

1. Automated pre-screening for spam, plagiarism, and abusive language.
2. Manual moderator evaluation for story credibility and evidence.
3. Publish after approval, or request revisions for improvement.
4. Reject stories that fail verification or violate policy.
5. Escalate suspicious funding or legal risk cases.

## 4. Verification Standards

- Check funding claims against uploaded receipts or pitch decks.
- Validate business identity when founders request investment support.
- Confirm that the story matches the claimed timeline and outcomes.
- Use third-party sources for high-risk cases when required.

## 5. Reporting and Appeals

- Users can report questionable stories or conduct.
- Moderators investigate reports within 24–48 hours.
- If a report is upheld, content may be removed and accounts reviewed.
- Founders can appeal moderation decisions with additional evidence.

## 6. Privacy and Confidentiality

- Allow anonymous publishing to protect founder identity.
- Permit founders to hide sensitive financial data.
- Store personal data securely and only use it for platform operations.
- Comply with data protection regulations such as GDPR.

## 7. Fraud Prevention

- Flag high-value funding requests for extra review.
- Monitor unusual posting or donation behavior.
- Require identity verification for major investors and large payouts.
- Use payment fraud tools for donations and crowdfunding.

## 8. Community Guidelines

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on learning, not blame.
- Support founders with actionable feedback.
- Avoid unsolicited attacks or personal criticism.
- Maintain a safe environment for sharing difficult experiences.
