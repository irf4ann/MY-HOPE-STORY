# Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Manual

## 1. Story Submission SOP

1. Entrepreneur logs in and creates/updates profile.
2. Complete the structured story form with required fields.
3. Upload media, documents, and evidence if available.
4. Set story visibility: public, private, anonymous.
5. Submit story for review.

## 2. Content Review SOP

1. AI review scans for spam, plagiarism, hate speech, and obvious fraud.
2. Moderator receives story in review queue.
3. Moderator checks basic facts, funding claims, and content quality.
4. If valid, moderator publishes the story.
5. If issues are found, moderator requests revisions or rejects the story.
6. Notify the founder with feedback and next steps.

## 3. Moderation Escalation SOP

1. Flagged story enters the high-risk queue for manual review.
2. Senior moderator validates claims and evidence.
3. If fraud or false fundraising is identified, suspend the story and user account.
4. Report severe cases to legal/compliance team.

## 4. Donation and Crowdfunding SOP

1. Once a founder activates funding, the platform verifies the campaign.
2. Set up campaign goal, deadline, and donation options.
3. Monitor donations and progress updates daily.
4. Release funds or transfer payments according to platform terms.
5. Send payment confirmation to donors and founders.

## 5. Mentor Matching SOP

1. Mentor signs up and completes expertise profile.
2. Platform recommends match based on industry, failure type, and needs.
3. Mentor and founder exchange introduction messages.
4. Mentor schedules sessions and records outcomes.

## 6. Investor Engagement SOP

1. Investor finds stories through search, filters, or recommendations.
2. Investor expresses interest via a secure connection form.
3. Platform notifies the founder and facilitates initial contact.
4. Investor and founder conduct due diligence off-platform.

## 7. Privacy and Data Handling SOP

1. Collect only necessary personal and business data.
2. Store sensitive data encrypted in transit and at rest.
3. Allow users to edit or delete personal profile data.
4. Honor anonymous publishing and hidden financial details.

## 8. Reporting and Feedback SOP

1. Enable users to report inappropriate content or suspicious campaigns.
2. Log reports and assign to moderators within 24 hours.
3. Provide feedback closure once the issue is resolved.

## 9. Platform Maintenance SOP

1. Monitor system health and security alerts daily.
2. Review analytics and metrics weekly.
3. Update moderation rules and content policies quarterly.
4. Implement feature releases through controlled deployments.
