# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## 1. Executive Summary

My Hope Story is a digital platform designed to capture founder failure stories, preserve lessons, and connect promising ideas with mentors and funding. It bridges storytelling, learning, community, and financial support for entrepreneurs.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Build the world’s largest library of documented startup failures.
- Increase transparency and reduce repeated entrepreneurial mistakes.
- Create pathways for failed founders to secure mentorship and funding.
- Support recovery and business revival for promising ideas.
- Enable data-driven insights into startup failure causes.

## 3. Stakeholders

- Entrepreneurs (primary)
- Investors and angel networks
- Mentors and advisors
- Students and business schools
- Incubators and accelerators
- Researchers and policymakers
- NGOs and government startup programs

## 4. User Personas

### 4.1 Failed Entrepreneur

- Age: 25–40
- Goals: share journey, learn from peers, secure support
- Needs: credibility, funding, mentorship, emotional validation

### 4.2 Investor

- Goals: identify quality ideas, evaluate founder resilience
- Needs: verified narratives, transparent failure reasons, business metrics

### 4.3 Student

- Goals: study real startup case studies
- Needs: structured lessons, accessible storytelling, searchability

### 4.4 Mentor

- Goals: guide founders, share expertise
- Needs: founder context, engagement tools, analytics

## 5. Problem Statement

Startups fail silently due to fear of judgment, loss of confidence, financial pressures, and reputational risk. Valuable lessons, incomplete ideas, and founder experiences disappear without a centralized platform.

## 6. Proposed Solution

A structured platform where founders submit verified failure stories, the community engages through comments and mentorship, and investors or supporters can fund revival efforts based on potential rather than perfection.

## 7. Key Features

- Registration and profile management
- Story creation and multimedia uploads
- Failure taxonomy and tagging
- Community interaction (comments, likes, shares)
- Search and recommendation engine
- Donation, crowdfunding, and investor connection systems
- Review and verification workflow
- Privacy controls and anonymous story options

## 8. Success Metrics

- Number of published failure stories
- Monthly active users
- Average story engagement rate
- Funding raised on the platform
- Number of mentor connections made
- Number of revived businesses
- User satisfaction and trust scores
